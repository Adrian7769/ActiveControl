// Hardware Debugging
Battery polarity — LIPO1 and LIPO2 positive terminals still appear to connect to GND and negative to +7.4V. This is backwards and will destroy everything. Double check this is correct before ordering anything.
FRAM address pins — MB85RC256V pins 6, 7, 8 (A0, A1, A2) appear unconnected. Tie all three to GND to set address 0x50 which matches your config.h.
FRAM WP pin — pin 3 looks unconnected. Tie to +3.3V to disable write protection.
I2C pull-up resistors — still missing. Four resistors, 4.7kΩ each, SDA and SCL to 3.3V on both buses.
TPS54308 inductor — L1 shows value 15, confirm units are µH not just 15.
GPIO4 for MOSFET — confirm the wire from R3 actually connects to GPIO4 on the ESP32, it's hard to tell at this zoom level.
RunCam VCC — pin 5 connection looks thin, zoom in and confirm it's actually connected.
